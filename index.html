META MAP

Learn the looks GeoGuessr locations fall into.

Level
WHAT IS THIS
  1. 2 million Street View locations from GeoGuessr-style coverage were compared by how they look, using a vision model.
  2. Locations that look alike were grouped. Each group is a meta: a look shared by many places, like "Kenyan red-dirt tracks" or "Mongolian roof-rack car".
  3. Four levels, each inside the one above: 40 regions (big areas that look alike), 141 sub-regions (smaller areas), 558 scenes (the kinds of road in each area: town streets, highways, rural roads, unpaved roads) and 4,272 looks (the finest detail).
  4. Explore a meta to see its photos, where it occurs and what it gets confused with. Practice to learn to recognize it from photos you have not seen.
  5. Hold Shift over any photo or dot to see it in all four directions.
